Ahlan Social

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Our Commitment

Ahlan Social is committed to protecting children from child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We prohibit the use of our platform to abuse, exploit, groom, or sexually target children. These standards apply to user accounts, posts, images, stories, direct messages, and other content and interactions on the platform.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Ahlan Social prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, including:

  • Creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming or attempting to manipulate a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual solicitation, exploitation, or trafficking involving minors.
  • Sexual extortion, threats, or blackmail involving minors.
  • Other conduct that sexually exploits children or facilitates child abuse.

3. Reporting Within the App

Ahlan Social provides an in-app reporting feature that allows users to report accounts and posts directly from the application. Users do not need to leave the app to submit a report.

The reporting menu includes the category "Nudity or sexual activity." This category can be used to report relevant sexual content or conduct, including suspected child sexual exploitation, inappropriate sexual interactions involving minors, and other related child-safety concerns.

Reports submitted through this feature appear in the Ahlan Social administration panel for review. The developer has tested this reporting workflow.

Reports are reviewed so that appropriate action can be taken based on the reported content, the available information, the severity of the concern, and applicable legal requirements.

4. Review and Enforcement

When Ahlan Social identifies content or conduct that violates these standards, appropriate action may include:

  • Reviewing reported accounts and content.
  • Removing content that violates these standards, including confirmed CSAM.
  • Restricting, suspending, or terminating accounts involved in violations.
  • Handling relevant information in accordance with applicable law.
  • Reporting cases to the appropriate authorities when required by law.

Ahlan Social does not permit the use of its platform to distribute or facilitate child sexual abuse material.

5. Compliance With Child Safety Laws

Ahlan Social is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations, including legal requirements concerning the handling and reporting of child sexual abuse material. Reports and suspected violations will be handled in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

6. Child Safety Contact

For questions about these standards, child-safety concerns, or Ahlan Social's child-safety practices, contact:

Email: ahlan.social@inbox.eu

This contact address is provided for child-safety matters and communications concerning compliance with applicable requirements.

7. Updates to These Standards

Ahlan Social may update these standards when necessary to improve child safety, clarify enforcement procedures, or address changes in applicable law. The latest version will be published at this same public URL.
